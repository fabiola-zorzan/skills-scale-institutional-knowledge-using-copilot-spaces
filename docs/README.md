# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

Welcome to the OctoAcme Project Management documentation hub. This README provides a summary of the key project management practices we use and links to detailed guides.

## Summary of Project Management Processes

OctoAcme follows a structured yet flexible project management approach designed to deliver customer value while maintaining clear ownership and measurable outcomes.

### Core Principles

- **Customer-first:** Prioritize user value and usability in projects. Every decision is evaluated through the lens of what benefits our customers most.

- **Iterative delivery:** Work is delivered in small, testable increments to enable rapid feedback and improvement. This approach reduces risk and allows teams to adapt quickly to changing requirements.

- **Clear ownership:** Each project has a named Project Manager and Product Lead. Clear roles ensure accountability and streamlined decision-making.

- **Evidence-based decisions:** Success and improvements are measured using defined metrics and retrospectives. Data drives our continuous improvement efforts.

- **Consistent documentation:** Artifacts such as one-pagers, risk registers, and retrospectives are kept up-to-date in this repository. Strong documentation ensures knowledge retention and team alignment.

### Key Characteristics

- Projects are initiated with clear objectives, stakeholder alignment, and resource planning
- Regular execution reviews track progress against defined metrics and milestones
- Risk management is proactive, with regular communication to stakeholders
- Release cycles follow established deployment and quality standards
- Post-project retrospectives capture lessons learned and drive process improvements
- Well-defined roles and personas ensure clarity across all project phases
